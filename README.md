Superstore Sales Data Analysis

Project Overview

This project explores retail sales data to identify sales trends, profitability patterns, customer segment performance, and opportunities for business improvement.

Dataset

- Records: 9,994
- Period: 2014–2017
- Source: Sample Superstore dataset

Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

Key Findings

- Technology generated the highest sales and total profit among the three main product categories.
- The Central region had the lowest regional profit margin at 7.92%.
- Tables recorded the largest sub-category loss, at $17,725.48.
- Home Office had the highest profit margin among customer segments, at 14.03%.
- November 2017 recorded the highest monthly sales, while December 2016 recorded the highest monthly profit.
- Several high-discount groups had negative average profit.

Business Recommendations

- Review pricing, costs, and discount policies for low-profit products.
- Investigate regional differences in profitability.
- Monitor sales and profit trends over time.
- Evaluate product performance using both sales and profit metrics.
